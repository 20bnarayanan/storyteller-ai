# Pimmerly Learns to Dance Into the Sky

Pimmerly was a shy green dragon with freckles scattered across her wings like spilled cinnamon. She lived in a cozy cave at the edge of Cinder Hill, and every night she danced there alone. Her tail swayed, her hips rolled, and her feet tapped out the quick, bright rhythm of salsa music. Her wings, however, stayed folded tight against her back. She was too **timid** to let anyone see them open.

One evening, a deep and steady thump drifted up the mountain. It was Abuela Quixelle, the elderly iguana who ran the dance hall, tapping her long tail on the stone floor. Everyone could hear it, even all the way down in the valley.

"The Festival of Lights is coming!" Abuela Quixelle announced. "Every dragon must rise into the lantern ring and dance one song in the air!"

Pimmerly's stomach flipped like a pancake in a hot pan. She sank lower on her cushion and hoped no one would notice her.

"Ha! Easy!" boasted Tobiah Bramblewick, a loud young dragon who could already loop three times in a row.

Pimmerly practiced all week with her friend Dotty Fennimore, a tiny moth who knew every secret in Cinder Hill. "Count with me," Dotty **fluttered**. "One, two, three, four! Dragons stay aloft when they keep a beat."

Pimmerly counted and stepped. She took a big breath and tried to lift off. But the moment her feet left the stone, they tangled, and her wings flopped like wet laundry. She tumbled into a heap.

Tobiah laughed so hard he nearly fell out of the air. After that, Pimmerly hid at the very back of every practice.

Then, late one night, she stepped out of her cave and gasped. The lanterns of the valley were going dark, one by one. Each had lost its warm **glimmer**, and soon the whole valley would be almost black.

"I have to find out what's happening," Pimmerly whispered. She followed the trail of fading light up the hill, into the cold, whistling wind.

At the top of the ridge, a stubborn gust swirled around and around, always swinging back to the same bare rock, snuffing out lantern after lantern. Pimmerly listened closely. The wind had a beat. Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh, like a steady drum.

It was just like counting salsa steps.

Pimmerly took a slow breath and counted, one, two, three, four. On the fourth beat, she spread her freckled wings and leaped. Her first flap wobbled, and she dipped toward the rocks. She held her breath, counted again, and pushed back up on the next beat. She drifted to the first dark lantern and breathed warm light into it. It flickered, then glowed.

She spun toward the second lantern, turning with the wind's whoosh. A gust shoved her sideways, and she dipped low, then rose on the beat again. Her wings found the rhythm, and she breathed into the second lantern until it shone gold.

Then the third. She swept around in a wide, bouncing circle, step, step, turn, and let her warm breath wrap around the wick until the light came alive. One dance step at a time, the whole ring of lanterns began to shine.

Down in the village, the crowd cheered as Pimmerly swooped and spun through the sky. Even Tobiah cheered loudest of all.

Later, as the wind settled and the lanterns burned steady, Dotty fluttered down beside her. "Step and breathe, and let the music carry you up," she sang softly.

*Step and breathe, and let the music carry you up.*

On the night of the Festival of Lights, Pimmerly danced a whole song in the air. Her nerves still buzzed like bees, but she kept stepping to her own beat, and she did not fall. Each spin felt a little less wobbly than the last, and the crowd clapped in time with Abuela Quixelle's tail.

Tobiah tried to loop three times, then grinned and gave her a nod. "Not bad, freckles," he said, and for once he did not laugh.

Back home in her cave, she folded her wings with a happy sigh. Being nervous was fine, as long as she kept moving to her own rhythm.

*Step and breathe, and let the music carry you.*

Her eyes grew heavy. The wind hummed a gentle tune, and the lanterns glowed soft and low.

Pimmerly yawned and tucked her tail beneath her chin.

*Step and breathe, and let the music go.*

The valley was quiet now. Only the faint hum of the wind remained.

Her freckled wings rested still against her back. Her breathing slowed.

The stars blinked above Cinder Hill. Somewhere far below, a last lantern kept its gentle light.

*Step and breathe.*

**Words we met tonight ✨**

- **Flutter:** to move the wings or hands quickly and lightly
- **Timid:** shy and easily frightened
- **Glimmer:** a soft, faint light that flickers

**Ask your little storyteller:** "When Pimmerly first heard the wind carrying the beat, what do you think she was feeling, and how did she choose to use it?"
